Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how "PRIME SOFT" LLC LLC ("we", "us", "the Company") collects, uses and protects personal data of visitors to this website and people who contact us.

2. What data we collect

  • Data you give us through the contact form or by email: your name, email address, the service you are interested in and the content of your message.
  • Data from business communication: information you share with us while we discuss or carry out a project, such as your company name, position and phone number.
  • Technical data: when you open the website, your browser automatically sends your IP address, browser type and the requested page to the hosting server and to Google Fonts, which loads the website's fonts.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data (health, religion, political views, etc.). Please do not send such data to us.

3. Why we use your data

  • to reply to your enquiry and prepare an estimate or proposal;
  • to conclude and perform contracts for our services;
  • to meet our legal obligations, including accounting and tax requirements;
  • to keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making and do not sell it to anyone.

4. Legal basis

We process personal data in accordance with the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection". Depending on the situation, processing is based on your consent (when you send us a message), on the need to prepare or perform a contract with you, on our legal obligations, or on our legitimate interest in running and protecting our business. Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, the same bases apply under Article 6 GDPR.

5. How the contact form works

The contact form does not store data on the website. When you press "Send", your own email program opens with a pre-filled message addressed to us. Your data reaches us only when you send that email.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider and Google Fonts may process technical data as described in section 2.

7. Who we share data with

We may share personal data only with:

  • service providers that help us run our business (hosting, email, accounting), under confidentiality obligations;
  • public authorities, when required by law.

If data is transferred outside Ukraine, we make sure an adequate level of protection is in place.

8. How long we keep data

Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are kept for up to 12 months. Data related to contracts is kept for the period of the contract and afterwards for as long as Ukrainian law requires, in particular for accounting and tax purposes.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • receive a copy of your data;
  • ask us to correct or delete your data;
  • object to processing or withdraw your consent at any time;
  • file a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights, or with the data protection authority of your country if GDPR applies.

To exercise these rights, write to support@primesoftllc.shop. We will reply within 30 days.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from loss, unauthorised access or disclosure. Access to your data is limited to employees who need it for their work.

11. Children

Our services are intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The current version is always published on this page with its effective date.
